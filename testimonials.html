From Concrete to Oasis: The Adebayo Family Story

The Adebayo family had a barren concrete backyard that was unusable during Abuja's hot seasons. They dreamed of a green space where their children could play and they could entertain guests.

Plant Affairs transformed their 500sqm space into a lush garden with native plants, a water feature, and shaded seating areas. The project was completed in just 6 weeks.

Greening the Workplace: Corporate Office Transformation

A leading corporate office in Lagos was experiencing low employee morale and high stress levels. Management decided to invest in office greening to improve the work environment.

Plant Affairs installed air-purifying plants throughout all 10 floors of the building. Post-installation surveys showed a 30% increase in employee satisfaction and a noticeable improvement in air quality.

Ngozi Okonjo

Business Owner, Lagos
