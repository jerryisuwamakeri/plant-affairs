Our Story

From Humble Beginnings to Abuja's Premier Plant Destination

Plant Affairs was founded in 2015 by Chinedu and Amina Okoro, two passionate plant enthusiasts who noticed a gap in the market for quality plants and professional gardening services in Abuja.

What started as a small nursery in their backyard has grown into Abuja's most trusted plant specialist, serving thousands of happy customers across the city and beyond.

Our journey has been guided by a simple philosophy: everyone deserves to experience the joy and benefits of plants in their lives, regardless of their gardening experience.

Our Journey

2015

The Beginning

Chinedu and Amina Okoro establish Plant Affairs as a small backyard nursery in Abuja, focusing on indoor plants for urban dwellers.

2016

First Retail Location

We open our first retail store in Wuse, Abuja, expanding our product range to include outdoor plants and basic gardening supplies.

2017

Landscaping Services Launch

We introduce professional landscaping services, helping clients transform their outdoor spaces with custom designs and expert installation.

2019

Expansion to Gwarinpa

We open our second location in Gwarinpa, increasing our capacity and expanding our service area across Abuja.

2020

E-commerce Platform

We launch our online store, making our plants and services accessible to customers across Nigeria with nationwide delivery.

2022

Sustainability Initiatives

We implement comprehensive sustainability practices, including water recycling, organic growing methods, and community education programs.

2023

Award Recognition
