Monthly Plant Care Schedule

Stay on top of your plant care with our monthly guide. These are general recommendations - adjust based on your specific plants and conditions.

July Plant Care Tasks

Increase Watering Frequency

With higher temperatures, most plants will need more frequent watering. Check soil moisture regularly.

Prune Spring Flowering Shrubs

Trim back shrubs that bloomed in spring to encourage new growth and better flowering next year.

Pest Monitoring

Keep an eye out for common summer pests like aphids and spider mites. Treat early to prevent infestations.

Fertilize Container Plants

Plants in containers benefit from regular feeding during their active growth period.

Provide Shade for Sensitive Plants

Some plants may need protection from intense midday sun during heatwaves.

This Month's Expert Tip

Watering in the Nigerian Heat

During hot periods, water your plants early in the morning or late in the evening to reduce evaporation. Focus water at the base of plants rather than on leaves to prevent fungal diseases.

By Dr. Adeola Johnson

Common Questions

Why are my plant's leaves turning yellow?

Yellow leaves can indicate overwatering, underwatering, or nutrient deficiencies. Check soil moisture and consider a balanced fertilizer.

How often should I repot my plants?

Most plants need repotting every 1-2 years. Look for roots growing through drainage holes or soil that dries out very quickly.
