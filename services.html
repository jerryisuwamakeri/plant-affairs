Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a typical landscaping project take?

The duration depends on the project scope. Small residential gardens typically take 1-2 weeks, while larger commercial projects may take 3-6 weeks. We provide a detailed timeline during the consultation phase.

Do you offer maintenance after installation?

Yes, we offer various maintenance packages from weekly to monthly visits. Our maintenance services include pruning, weeding, fertilizing, pest control, and seasonal plant care.

What areas in Abuja do you serve?

We serve all areas of Abuja including Wuse, Gwarinpa, Maitama, Asokoro, Jabi, and surrounding communities. Additional travel fees may apply for locations outside our primary service area.

Can you work with my existing garden plants?

Absolutely! We assess the health and suitability of existing plants and incorporate them into our design when possible. We can also recommend relocation or propagation if needed.

What is your plant replacement policy?

We offer a 30-day health guarantee on all plants we install. If any plant fails within this period due to nursery-related issues, we replace it at no additional cost.
