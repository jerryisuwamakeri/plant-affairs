Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about contacting us and our services.

What's the best way to contact you for urgent plant issues?

For urgent plant health issues, we recommend calling our main line at +234 801 234 5678 during business hours. For after-hours emergencies, use our emergency line at +234 803 456 7890.

Do you offer virtual consultations?

Yes, we offer virtual consultations via video call for customers who cannot visit our nursery in person. These sessions include plant assessment, care recommendations, and design advice. Book through our website or by phone.

How quickly do you respond to contact form submissions?

We typically respond to contact form submissions within 4 hours during business hours. For inquiries submitted outside business hours, we respond by the next business day.

Can I visit your nursery without an appointment?

Yes, our nurseries are open to the public during business hours. However, for personalized consultations with our specialists, we recommend scheduling an appointment to ensure availability.

Do you service areas outside Abuja?

While our physical nurseries are in Abuja, we offer nationwide delivery for plants and supplies. For landscaping services, we primarily serve the Abuja area but may consider projects in neighboring states depending on scope.
