Our Hiring Process

1

Application Review

Our hiring team reviews all applications carefully. We look for both skills and cultural fit.

2

Initial Interview

A phone or video call to discuss your experience and interest in the role.

3

Skills Assessment

Depending on the role, you may complete a practical task or assessment.

4

In-Person Interview

Meet with the hiring manager and potential team members at one of our locations.

5

Offer & Onboarding

Successful candidates receive an offer and begin our comprehensive onboarding process.

We aim to complete the hiring process within 2-3 weeks and provide updates at every stage.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need previous experience with plants to work at Plant Affairs?

While plant knowledge is beneficial for some roles, we provide comprehensive training for all positions. What's most important is a willingness to learn and a passion for our mission.

What career progression opportunities are available?

We're committed to internal promotion and career development. Many of our managers started in entry-level positions. We offer regular training and clear paths for advancement.

Do you offer flexible or remote work arrangements?

While most of our roles require presence at our locations, we offer flexible scheduling where possible. Some corporate roles may have hybrid options.

What is the company culture like?

We have a collaborative, supportive culture focused on continuous learning and environmental stewardship. Teamwork and customer satisfaction are at the heart of everything we do.
